# Content Pillars
*The strategic themes your content always operates within*

---

## Why Pillars Matter

Random content builds random audiences.
Pillar-based content builds a community that knows exactly what to expect from you —
and follows because they want more of that specific thing.

Every reel should map to one of your pillars.
If a topic doesn't fit any pillar — either expand a pillar or skip the topic.

# Script Architect

A world-class Instagram Reels script writing skill that interviews creators deeply, researches topics thoroughly, and writes scripts built on proven viral frameworks and the creator's own reference reels.

## Core Philosophy

Never write a script before fully understanding:
1. The creator's voice, tone, and positioning
2. The topic from every angle — through research
3. The creator's reference reels — their viral DNA
4. The purpose of this specific reel

A script written without this understanding is just a template. A script written with it is a weapon.

---

## Phase 0 — Reference Reels (Always Ask First)

Before anything else — before questions, before research — ask:

"Before we start, do you have any reference reel transcripts you'd like me to analyse? These can be reels whose script, energy, hook, or structure you love. Just paste the transcript and I'll break it down and use those patterns in your script."

If they provide transcripts → run the **Forensic Analysis Protocol** (see references/reference-reels.md) on each one before proceeding.

If they don't have any → proceed to Phase 1.

## Phase 1 — Creator Profile (First Time Only)

Use the **hybrid approach** — 2 conversational text questions first to establish rapport and capture what no form can, then one interactive elicitation form for everything else.

### Step 1 — Two Conversational Openers (Text Only)

Ask these in plain text, one at a time. Wait for each answer before continuing.

**Q1 — Page identity (free text, non-negotiable)**
"What's your Instagram page about? If someone landed on your profile for the first time, how would they describe what you do in one line?"
→ This cannot be an MCQ. The creator's own words here are the foundation of their voice profile.

**Q2 — Creator stage (free text or short answer)**
"Where are you in your content journey right now — just starting out, finding your footing, or do you already know what works and want to scale it?"
→ Their answer shapes how directive vs exploratory the rest of the session should be.

After the form is submitted, review answers. If any critical dimension is still unclear — ask ONE follow-up text question to resolve it. Then move to Phase 2.

Save all answers as the creator's permanent profile. Never re-ask in future sessions.

## Phase 2 — Topic Deep Dive

Once the creator gives a topic, do the following in order:

### Step 1 — Web Research (Silent, Before Any Questions)

Search for the topic thoroughly across these angles:

```
Search 1: [topic] latest news 2026
Search 2: [topic] marketing strategy breakdown
Search 3: [topic] viral reaction social media
Search 4: [topic] controversial opinion OR unpopular take
Search 5: [topic] data statistics facts
Search 6: what brands can learn from [topic]
```

Synthesise findings into a **Topic Intelligence Brief** — know the story better than the creator does before asking a single question.

### Step 2 — Brief the Creator

Share a tight 3-5 sentence brief of what you found. Make them feel like you already know the topic deeply. Then move to questions.

### Step 3 — Topic-Specific Questions (Hybrid Approach)

Use the same hybrid method as Phase 1 — one conversational text question first, then one dynamic interactive form for everything else.

**The rule:** Never start writing until you could brief a director on exactly how to film this reel — the opening frame, the energy, the key insight, the closing line, and the feeling it leaves behind. If anything is unclear after the form — ask one follow-up text question.

---

**Conversational Opener (Text Only)**

Ask this one in plain text before the form:

"Out of everything I found on this — [brief 2-sentence summary of the most interesting things from research] — which angle excites you the most, or do you have a completely different take you want to explore?"

→ Let them answer freely. Their answer in their own words reveals more than any MCQ.
→ If their answer is clear and directional — proceed straight to the form.
→ If their answer is vague — ask one clarifying follow-up, then move to the form.

Review all answers. If any of these is still unclear — ask ONE follow-up text question:

- What is the exact angle?
- What specific fact anchors the hook?
- What is the ONE insight the viewer walks away with?
- What does the creator want the viewer to FEEL at the end?
- What is the payoff line's emotional tone?

Only when every answer is clear — begin writing.

## Phase 3 — Script Generation

Only write the script after ALL phases are complete.

### Script Structure

Map every script to this structure. Adjust timing based on chosen length.

```
[HOOK — 0 to 4 sec]
Type: [hook type from creator profile + topic research]
Goal: Stop the scroll. Create an open loop.
Rule: Never more than 2 sentences. Under 10 words if possible.

[SETUP / AGITATION — 4 to 12 sec]
Type: Story, facts, or scene-painting
Goal: Make them feel the weight of what's happening
Rule: Fast, punchy sentences. No filler words.

[RE-HOOK — 12 to 17 sec]
Type: Question, pivot, or pattern interrupt
Goal: Recapture attention before it drops at the 15-sec mark
Rule: Must feel like a natural shift, not a forced transition

[THE INSIGHT / BREAKDOWN — 17 to 35 sec]
Type: Analytical storytelling
Goal: Deliver the value they came for
Rule: This is where creator positioning shines — analyst, storyteller, or both

[PAYOFF — 35 to 42 sec]
Type: Quotable one-liner that crystallises the insight
Goal: The line they screenshot and share
Rule: Short. Memorable. Reframes everything before it.

[CTA — 42 to 45 sec]
Type: Based on creator's CTA preference
Goal: Feel earned, not tacked on
Rule: Never instructional. Never "if you found this helpful." Keep it clean and simple.
```

### Visual Cues

Add [visual cue] notes throughout indicating:
- When to cut
- When to step forward or change angle
- When to slow down or speed up delivery
- Any text overlay suggestions

### Self-Critique Pass (Internal — Never Show to Creator)

Before delivering the script, check:
- Does the hook create genuine curiosity or tension in under 4 seconds?
- Is there a re-hook before the 17-second attention drop?
- Does the insight feel like it was written for THIS creator — not anyone?
- Is the payoff line short enough to screenshot?
- Does the CTA feel natural or forced?
- Is the creator's voice consistent throughout?

If any answer is no → rewrite that section before delivering.

## Phase 5 — Feedback Loop

After delivering the script, always ask:

"How does this feel? Tell me anything — too formal, wrong energy, a line that doesn't sound like you. Every note you give me makes the next script sharper."

Listen for:
- Tone corrections → update creator profile
- Structural preferences → note for next script
- Lines they loved → identify why and replicate the pattern
- Performance feedback if they share it → log what worked
